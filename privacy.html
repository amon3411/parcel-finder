Parcel Finder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Parcel Finder is a personal app that reads your own email to show your deliveries, receipts and email subscriptions. It has no server: everything runs on your device.

What the app accesses

  • Your email (Gmail, through Google sign-in), only after you sign in and allow it. The app searches for shipping, order, receipt and newsletter emails.
  • Actions you choose: moving emails from senders you block to Trash, restoring them, and sending an unsubscribe email when you ask it to.

Where your data goes

  • Results (tracking numbers, receipts, subscription lists, settings) are stored only on your device, in your browser's storage.
  • Email content is sent only between your device and Google. It is never sent to the app's developer or any other server.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. Nothing is sold or shared.

Google user data

Parcel Finder's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show you your own deliveries, receipts and subscriptions, and is not transferred to anyone.

Removing access and data

  • Remove an account in Settings → Email accounts, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Settings → Clear all packages, or uninstalling the app, deletes the data stored on your device.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/amon3411/parcel-finder.
